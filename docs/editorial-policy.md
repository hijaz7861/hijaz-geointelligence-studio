# Editorial Policy

1. Record the original source URL and publication date for every factual claim.
2. Label claims as verified fact, official statement, independent reporting,
   analysis, or unverified information.
3. Seek independent corroboration for consequential claims.
4. State uncertainty and disagreements clearly.
5. Use only footage and audio with documented permission or a suitable license.
6. Prefer original narration, graphics, maps, and explanatory animation.
7. Do not include real-time targeting, actionable attack guidance, or operational
   instructions that could facilitate violence.
8. Require a human editor to approve scripts, translations, media rights, and
   the final video before publication.
