# Why world maps show borders differently

[EDITORIAL] A political map is not a neutral window. Every map is built for a purpose, at a particular scale, with a particular boundary policy.

[[claim:Natural Earth says its Admin 0 countries layer shows de facto boundaries by default, reflecting who controls territory rather than de jure claims.]] Natural Earth says its Admin 0 countries layer shows de facto boundaries by default, reflecting who controls territory rather than de jure claims.

[EDITORIAL] That is a mapping convention, not a final legal judgment. Before interpreting a border, check the dataset, its scale, its date, and how it treats disputed areas.
