The bundled country-boundary dataset is derived from Natural Earth, which describes its vector datasets as public domain.

- Natural Earth dataset description: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

The GeoJSON conversion repository's README says it provides Natural Earth data converted to GeoJSON and characterizes the underlying Natural Earth vector dataset as public domain. This is provenance evidence, not a legal opinion or a guarantee that every upstream repository artifact has identical terms. Review Natural Earth's current terms before external/commercial redistribution.

This bundled dataset is generalized to 1:110m scale. It is appropriate for world-scale illustrative/cinematic previews, not navigation, surveying, boundary adjudication, or precision geospatial analysis. Country boundaries and disputed areas may reflect cartographic conventions and are not authoritative legal boundaries.
